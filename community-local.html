Local Philadelphia Groups

These groups are based in Philadelphia and focused on the local birding community. All are welcoming to beginners.

BirdPhilly

BirdPhilly is a program of the Delaware Valley Ornithological Club focused specifically on birding within Philadelphia city limits. It runs a year-round schedule of free guided field trips in partnership with Philadelphia Parks & Recreation and the Fairmount Park Conservancy. Trips are open to all ages and experience levels and are led by expert local birders. BirdPhilly is one of the best entry points for beginner birders in the city.

Visit BirdPhilly

In Color Birding Club

In Color Birding Club was founded to encourage and support nature lovers of color in the Philadelphia area. The club organizes birding outings, community events, and educational programming with a focus on making birding welcoming and accessible for BIPOC communities. Their walks are beginner-friendly and regularly take place at FDR Park and Pennypack on the Delaware.

Visit In Color Birding Club

Philly Queer Birders

Philly Queer Birders is an inclusive birding group created to provide a welcoming space for LGBTQ+ birders in Philadelphia. Open to anyone who shares their values, the group organizes casual outings and community events across the city with the goal of creating an intentional space where queer people feel welcomed and supported.

Visit Philly Queer Birders

Feminist Bird Club Philly

Feminist Bird Club Philly is a collaborative, community-oriented birding group that emphasizes inclusivity, conversation, and shared ownership of the birding experience. The club partners with other local environmental organizations and hosts regular walks and meetings throughout the year.
