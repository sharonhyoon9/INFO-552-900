Regional Organizations

These larger organizations serve the greater Philadelphia and Delaware Valley region and offer resources, memberships, and programming beyond the city limits.

Delaware Valley Ornithological Club (DVOC)

Founded in 1890, the DVOC is one of the oldest ornithological organizations in the United States. Members meet at the Academy of Natural Sciences and the club publishes the journal Cassinia. The DVOC organizes field trips across the Delaware Valley region and runs BirdPhilly for Philadelphia-focused outings. Membership is open to anyone with an interest in birds.

Visit DVOC

Pennsylvania Center for Adapted Sports — Adapted Birding

The Pennsylvania Center for Adapted Sports runs an Adapted Birding program designed to make birding accessible to people with mobility needs and disabilities. The program organizes accessible outings at Philadelphia-area locations and provides support and equipment to participants.

Visit PCAS

Valley Forge Audubon Society

The Valley Forge Audubon Society serves the greater Philadelphia region with field trips, conservation programming, and educational events. They maintain a comprehensive guide to birding hotspots in Philadelphia and surrounding counties, which is a useful resource for planning outings beyond the city limits.

Visit Valley Forge Audubon

Pennsylvania Society for Ornithology

The Pennsylvania Society for Ornithology is the statewide organization for birders in Pennsylvania. It maintains a directory of regional birding clubs across the state and publishes resources for Pennsylvania birders of all levels.

Visit PSO
