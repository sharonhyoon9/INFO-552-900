Purpose Statement

This portfolio was created to practice my skills utilizing HTML and CSS. My work aims to preserve material records of artistic culture and history so that researchers, communities, and future generations can access and learn from it.

Professional Philosophy Statement

I believe that meaningful access to cultural heritage depends on the quality of the systems and metadata that underpin it. The best collections work is often invisible to the end user— I'd like to bridge together cultural heritage institutions and their collections to the public.

The four pillars that drive my work are accessibility, preservation, discovery, and use.
