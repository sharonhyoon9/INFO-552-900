About The Philadelphia Birder

Birdwatching, or “birding”, is an extremely popular hobby that refers to the activity of observing birds. This website focuses on the Philadelphia birding community as a whole, but with a particular focus on beginner birders. While there are “levels” to birding, a community website that provides resources and information about the hobby within a local context will be beneficial for all. The Philadelphia Birder exists to consolidate existing birding resources for Philadelphia County residents in one accessible, beginner-friendly place. Philadelphia has a vibrant and growing birding community, but information about local spots, species, clubs, and events is scattered across dozens of websites and social media groups, with no single entry point for someone just getting started.

Whether you have never picked up a pair of binoculars or are an experienced birder looking for a local sources, The Philadelphia Birder is designed to be useful to you!

What You Will Find Here

  • Getting Started : general introduction to birding and navigating this website
  • Birding Spots : a guide to birding locations within Philadelphia
  • Species Guide : common birds found in Philadelphia with additional resources
  • Community & Clubs : local birding organizations and groups in Philadelphia
  • Events : regularly scheduled events and birding gatherings
  • Resources : recommended apps, books, websites, films, and contacts

A Note on the Images on this Site

The ornithological illustrations used throughout this site are from John James Audubon's "Birds of America"; while these images are public domain, the digital files are from the Audubon. All images on this site are provided courtesy of the John James Audubon Center at Mill Grove in Audubon, Pennsylvania; the Montgomery County Audubon Collection; and Zebra Publishing.

These images are also a reminder that people's interest in birds has a long and rich history. As historical illustrations, they may not reflect accurate or current species or features and are not intended to be used for identification.

The Philadelphia Birder is a community resource for beginner birders in Philadelphia, PA.
