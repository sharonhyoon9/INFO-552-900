About Me

I am currently a museum worker in Philadelphia pursuing an MLIS with a metadata and digital technologies certificate.

I studied art history and philosophy during undergrad. My art interests include 19th century American Art and historical East Asian Art. I do a bit of freelance provenance research and collections management on the side as well!

I currently work as a Museum Collections Assistant at the Pennsylvania Academy of the Fine Arts. My position encompasses four different departments: the Museum, Archives, Library, and School. However, after nearly 3 years here, I will be starting a new position at the Philadelphia Museum of Art on June 1!

I enjoy reading nonfiction, film photography, and traveling. In the past year, I've been lucky enough to go on a roadtrip through the Pacific Northwest with friends and take a solo trip to Prague and Vienna!
