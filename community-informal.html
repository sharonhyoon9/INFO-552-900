Informal Communities

Not ready to join a formal club? These online spaces and informal communities are great low-pressure ways to connect with other birders, share sightings, learn, and ask beginner questions.

eBird — Philadelphia County

eBird is the world's largest citizen science platform for bird sightings, run by the Cornell Lab of Ornithology. Philadelphia birders regularly log sightings on eBird, making it an excellent resource for finding out what has been seen recently and where. You can explore the Philadelphia County hotspot map to see recent sightings at local parks and plan your outings accordingly.

eBird Philadelphia

Reddit — r/birding

The r/birding subreddit is one of the most active online birding communities. It is a welcoming space for beginners to ask identification questions, share sightings, and learn from experienced birders. While not Philadelphia-specific, many local birders participate and regional posts are common.

r/birding on Reddit

iNaturalist — Philadelphia

iNaturalist is a citizen science platform for logging observations of all wildlife, including birds. The Philadelphia community on iNaturalist is active and a good complement to eBird for connecting with local naturalists more broadly. Observations can be identified by the community, making it a useful tool for beginners unsure of what they have spotted.

iNaturalist Philadelphia

Free Library of Philadelphia — Birder Backpack Program

The Free Library of Philadelphia offers birding backpacks for loan at several branch locations. Each backpack includes binoculars and a field guide. This is a great option for beginners who want to try birding before investing in equipment.
