Getting Started

What is Birding?

Birding, or birdwatching, is the hobby of observing birds in their natural habitats. It can be as casual as noticing the birds in your backyard or as dedicated as planning detailed trips to try and find birds. According to a 2022 demographic and economic analysis conducted by the U. S. Fish & Wildlife Service, over 1/3 of the population (96 million individuals!) are birders. There are no rules to birding; you can bird alone or with a group, in a park or on your commute, with expensive equipment or none at all.

Birding is one of the most accessible outdoor hobbies available. It requires little to no financial investment to get started and can be done almost anywhere. Philadelphia, with its parks and position along the Atlantic Flyway, is a great place to bird.

What You Need

The good news is that you don't need much to get started. Here is what helps:

  • Your eyes and ears : the most important tools you have. Many birds are heard before they are seen.
  • Binoculars : not required, but helpful.
  • A field guide or app : the Merlin Bird ID app (free, by the Cornell Lab of Ornithology) is the best starting point for beginners. It can identify birds by photo or sound and works well in Philadelphia. Many birders prefer to log the birds they see, and an app like eBird is widely used for that purpose. See our Resources page for more recommendations.
  • Comfortable clothes and shoes : dress accordingly and comfortably for the weather!
  • Patience : birds don't always cooperate, and that is part of the fun.

Etiquette

Birding has a few informal rules that help protect birds and make the experience better for everyone:

  • Stay on designated trails and avoid disturbing nesting areas.
  • Keep noise to a minimum, as loud sounds can scare birds away.
  • Do not use playback (playing recorded bird calls) excessively.
  • Be respectful of other birders and nature.
  • Leave no trace!

How to Use This Website

The Philadelphia Birder is organized around the most common questions beginner birders have. Here is a quick guide to what you will find:

  • Birding Spots : Not sure where to go? There are a number of great birding locations within Philadelphia County.
  • Species Guide : Curious about what birds you might see? Browse common species you might find in Philadelphia and beyond.
  • Community & Clubs : Want to bird with others? Find local groups, clubs, and organizations here.
  • Events : Looking for something to do? Check out upcoming walks and birding events in the city.
  • Resources : Find recommended apps, books, and websites for further information.
